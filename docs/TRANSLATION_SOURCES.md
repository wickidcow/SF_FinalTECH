# Translation sources

English terminology in this fork is cross-checked against the established FinalTECH lineage before new wording is introduced.

Primary reference:
- `LobbyTech-MC/FinalTech` — established English item, machine, and progression terminology.

Secondary references may be used for historical behavior or renamed mechanics, but the Changed fork remains the gameplay baseline. Translation work must not silently replace Changed-specific balance, recipes, mechanics, IDs, or saved-data contracts with behavior from another port.
